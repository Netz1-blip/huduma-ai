## SERVICE 9: HELB (Higher Education Loans Board)

### What is it
The Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) was created in 1995 (CAP 213A). It provides loans, scholarships, and bursaries to Kenyans in universities, TVETs, and other recognised institutions. All loans must be repaid with interest. Since 2023, new students fall under the **Student‑Centred Funding Model**, which combines a government scholarship (via the Universities Fund) and a HELB loan. New applicants use **www.hef.co.ke**; the older HELB portal (www.helb.co.ke) is for continuing students, Jielimishe loans, and repayment.

### Quick Facts
- **Loan application fee**: FREE
- **Portal for new students (undergraduate/TVET)**: www.hef.co.ke
- **Portal for continuing students, Jielimishe, repayment**: www.helb.co.ke
- **Need**: National ID (or KCSE index number if under 18), admission letter, active bank account, 2 guarantors, KRA PIN (student + parents)
- **Interest rates**: Undergraduate/TVET – **4% p.a.**; Jielimishe – **10% p.a.**
- **Grace period**: **1 year** after completing studies (undergraduate/TVET); Jielimishe repayment starts immediately
- **M‑Pesa Paybill**: **200800** (account number: your National ID)
- **USSD**: ***642#** (balance, repayment)
- **Compliance Certificate**: FREE for fully repaid loanees; **KES 1,000** for non‑beneficiaries
- **Penalty for late payment**: 5% of overdue instalment per month (minimum **KES 5,000**)
- **HELB Call Centre**: **0711 052 000**

### Key Reality Checks
- **Portals**: HEF/HELB portals frequently crash or go under maintenance during peak application season. Applications often get stuck on "pending".
- **Disbursement**: Tuition and upkeep are frequently delayed beyond the official 30‑day window. Some students are skipped entirely, leading to exam blocks.
- **Loan amounts**: The combined scholarship + loan often leaves a tuition shortfall; upkeep is widely considered too low for realistic living costs.
- **MTI classification**: Many students complain of misclassification in the means‑testing process, feeling they were unfairly placed in lower‑need bands.
- **Compliance Certificate**: Portal errors and backlogs are common; many graduates find records incorrectly show arrears due to employer non‑remittance.
- **Repayment**: CRB listing and debt collection are aggressive. There is no clear, published process for payment holidays or hardship relief.
- **Employer check‑off**: Some employers deduct but never remit, leaving the graduate in default.

## PART A: TYPES OF HELB LOANS & WHO QUALIFIES

HELB offers several products. All require you to be a Kenyan citizen with a valid National ID (or KCSE index number if under 18).

- **Undergraduate First‑Time Loan** – For students placed by KUCCPS in public or private universities. Award based on the Means Testing Instrument (MTI) on the HEF portal. Covers partial tuition and upkeep. Interest: **4% p.a.**
- **Undergraduate Subsequent Loan** – For continuing students who already received a first‑time loan. Apply annually via HEF/HELB.
- **TVET Loan** – For students in approved TVET institutions. Similar structure to undergraduate. Interest: **4% p.a.**
- **Jielimishe Loan** – For salaried employees pursuing professional diplomas, degrees, or postgraduate studies. Must be in formal employment. Maximum award up to **KES 600,000** (subject to one‑third of net salary). Interest: **10% p.a.** Repayment starts immediately.
- **Postgraduate Scholarship** – Competitive, non‑refundable. Covers **KES 200,000** (Masters) or **KES 450,000** (PhD) for up to 2–3 years. Application fee: **KES 3,000**.
- **Afya Elimu Fund** – For medical training college students (KMTC, etc.). Covers tuition and upkeep based on MTI.
- **Other funds** – Bar Examination Loan (BEL), KASNEB Foundation Scheme, Maritime Education Training Scheme, various county partnership funds.

**Important:** Under the new model, a student receives a combination of **Government Scholarship (Universities Fund) + HELB Loan**. The scholarship pays tuition directly to the university; the loan covers the remaining tuition top‑up and upkeep. All new applications go through **www.hef.co.ke**.

## PART B: ELIGIBILITY & GUARANTOR REQUIREMENTS

**Eligibility:**
- Kenyan citizen (birth or registration).
- Placed in a recognized Kenyan university or TVET institution by KUCCPS or a recognized placement agency.
- For undergrad/TVET first‑time: KCSE certificate and admission letter.
- Have a valid National ID; if under 18 and no ID, use your KCSE index number.
- Active bank account in your name for upkeep.
- KRA PIN (student + parents/guardians recommended).
- At least **two guarantors**.

**Guarantor requirements – the reality:**
- Must be a Kenyan citizen with a valid National ID.
- Should be in formal employment or have a verifiable income. HELB often rejects guarantors who are students, unemployed, or on irregular small incomes.
- Must not be a co‑applicant (another student applying that same year).
- Must be willing to sign the loan agreement and be contactable.
- In practice, students use: parents/relatives with jobs, high school teachers, pastors, local civil servants, or small business owners with a track record. If you can't find a guarantor, visit a HELB office or ask your institution's financial aid office for advice.

## PART C: HOW TO APPLY

**Critical:** The **number‑one cause of failed applications is using the wrong portal**. Follow the guide for your category exactly.

### For First‑Time Undergraduate / TVET Students (New Funding Model)
**Use:** **www.hef.co.ke**

1. **Prepare documents offline** before going online:
   - National ID (or KCSE index number if under 18)
   - Admission letter from your institution
   - Parents'/guardians' National ID copies (or death certificate if deceased)
   - Two guarantors' National ID copies
   - A clear digital passport‑size photo
   - Your bank account details (must be in your name)
   - KRA PIN (yours and ideally your parents')
   - If orphaned or from a single‑parent household, prepare supporting documents (chief's letter, death certificate)

2. **Go to www.hef.co.ke** (use **Chrome or Firefox** on a desktop; mobile phones often fail during uploads).

3. **Register** with your National ID or KCSE index number. Use an email address you can access – check the spam folder for the activation link.

4. **Fill in the Means Testing Instrument (MTI) carefully:** This decides your need band, scholarship amount, and loan amount. Include all household members, dependants, parents' income (or lack of it), and special circumstances. False or incomplete information can lead to reclassification or outright rejection.

5. **Upload clear scanned copies** (PDF or JPEG, under the size limit).

6. **Accept the scholarship and loan terms** shown by the system.

7. **Submit** – save or print the confirmation page.

8. **Track** your status on the same portal. "Pending" for days is normal during peak season; if there's no update for over two weeks, call HELB or visit a Huduma Centre.

**Avoid cyber café errors:** If you use a cyber, ensure the attendant uses **YOUR email, YOUR phone number, and YOUR correct bank details**.

### For Continuing Students, Jielimishe, and Repayment
**Use:** **www.helb.co.ke** → Student Portal or Loanees Portal.
- Continuing students apply annually when the window opens.
- Jielimishe applicants upload: admission letter, pay slips, employment letter, employer's details.
- The process is similar: register/login, fill forms, upload, submit.

## PART D: HOW THE FUNDING WORKS (AND WHY YOU MAY STILL OWE MONEY)

The old flat‑rate loan is gone. The current system:

1. **You apply once on the HEF portal.**
2. **Universities Fund awards a scholarship** covering **30%–70%** of tuition based on your MTI score:
   - Highest need: up to 70%
   - Moderate need: up to 50%
   - Lower need: up to 30%
3. **HELB awards a loan** to cover the rest of the tuition and provide upkeep.
   - A fixed portion (about **KES 8,000 per year**) goes to tuition.
   - The rest goes to your bank as upkeep (total loan range roughly **KES 37,000–60,000**, but after tuition deduction the actual upkeep received is less).
4. **You and your family** are expected to cover the remaining shortfall.

**Example:** If your course costs KES 120,000 and you are high need (70% scholarship):
- Scholarship covers KES 84,000 (sent to university).
- HELB loan provides KES 40,000: KES 8,000 to tuition (total tuition covered = KES 92,000) and KES 32,000 as upkeep.
- **You must still find KES 28,000** from family or other sources.

If you believe your MTI classification is wrong, contact HELB or the Universities Fund with supporting evidence (chief's letter, income proof). **Results vary.**

## PART E: DISBURSEMENT – WHEN THE MONEY ARRIVES (AND WHAT TO DO IF IT DOESN'T)

- **Tuition:** Sent to your institution's bank account.
- **Upkeep:** Sent to your personal bank account.

**Official timeline:** Within **30 days** of the semester start.  
**Real timeline:** Frequently delayed by weeks. Many students begin the term with no upkeep, and some are skipped entirely.

**If your upkeep hasn't arrived:**
1. Check your HELB/HEF portal status. Does it say "disbursed"?
2. If "disbursed", confirm with your bank – the delay may be on the bank's side.
3. If "disbursed" but the university says they haven't received tuition, get a disbursement confirmation (screenshot or letter) and present it to the university finance office.
4. If "pending" or "not disbursed", contact HELB: **0711 052 000**, contactcentre@helb.co.ke, or visit a Huduma Centre HELB desk.

**Consequence of non‑disbursement:** Universities can block exams, registration, or graduation. Always keep records of your HELB communication.

## PART F: REPAYMENT – RULES AND REALITY

### When repayment starts
- **Undergraduate/TVET:** **1 year** after you finish your course (grace period), or as soon as you begin earning, whichever comes first.
- **Jielimishe:** Immediately.

### How to repay
- **M‑Pesa Paybill:** **200800**, account number = your National ID. Keep every M‑Pesa confirmation message.
- **USSD:** ***642#** → Loan Repayment → M‑Pesa → enter amount.
- **HELB Mobile App** (Android) – balance and payments.
- **Bank deposit** into HELB accounts (KCB, Co‑op, Equity – full list on www.helb.co.ke).
- **Employer check‑off** – deducted from salary. Check your HELB statement quarterly to ensure the money was actually remitted.

### Penalties and interest
- **Interest:** **4% p.a.** (undergraduate/TVET); **10% p.a.** (Jielimishe).
- **Late payment penalty:** **5%** of the overdue instalment per month, subject to a minimum of **KES 5,000**. This applies to missed instalments, not the entire balance.
- **Default consequences:** CRB listing, debt collectors, legal action.

### If you can't repay
There is **no automatic deferment**. You must contact HELB directly (visit a branch or email) with evidence of your situation. They may grant an instalment plan or temporary reprieve, but interest continues to accrue. Ignoring HELB while in default leads to CRB listing and can block future employment.

## PART G: HELB COMPLIANCE CERTIFICATE

This certificate proves you are either a non‑loanee or an up‑to‑date repayer. **Most formal employers now demand it before hiring.**

### How to get it
- **Fully repaid your loan:** Apply via www.helb.co.ke → Compliance Certificate. **FREE**. You receive a Clearance Certificate.
- **Never took a HELB loan:** Apply via the portal. Pay **KES 1,000**. You receive a Compliance Certificate showing you are a non‑beneficiary.
- **Still repaying but up‑to‑date:** You can get a Compliance Certificate (status: compliant) for **FREE**.

### Pain points
- **Portal errors:** Applications fail, stay "pending", or the system incorrectly shows arrears.
- **Backlogs:** During peak hiring seasons, wait times can be weeks.
- **Incorrect records:** If your employer deducted but didn't remit, HELB sees you as a defaulter. Resolve this by providing proof of deductions (payslips) to both HELB and your employer.
- **If in default:** No certificate is issued. You must negotiate a payment plan and become current first.

## PART H: ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT & STATEMENTS

- **Check loan balance:** www.helb.co.ke Loanees Portal, ***642#**, or HELB mobile app.
- **Get a loan statement:** Download from the portal or email contactcentre@helb.co.ke. Some Huduma Centre cyber cafés assist for a small fee (KES 100–200).
- **Update personal details:** Keep your employer, address, and phone number current on the portal. HELB uses these to trace you.

---

## PART I: DEATH, DISABILITY, AND LOAN WAIVERS

- **Death:** The loan is covered by the Students' Loan Self‑Protection Scheme (SLSPS). Next of kin must contact HELB with a death certificate and complete a claim form. **The process is manual and can take many months.** Follow up persistently.
- **Permanent disability:** Also covered by SLSPS. Requires medical certification. Claims face similar delays.

---

## PART J: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Using the wrong portal (HEF for new students, HELB for old/Jielimishe).
- Letting a cyber café use their own email, phone, or bank details for your application.
- Filling the MTI form carelessly – it determines your need band and funding.
- Ignoring HELB after graduation – engage early to avoid default and CRB listing.
- Assuming your employer is remitting – check your HELB statement at least every 3–4 months.
- Forgetting to update contact details – HELB will consider you "unreachable".
- Not keeping M‑Pesa payment confirmations.

## PART K: COMMON QUESTIONS (BILINGUAL)

### Swahili
Q: Baada ya kumaliza shule, sina kazi. Je, lazima nilipe HELB?
A: HELB inatoa muda wa mwaka mmoja kabla ya kuanza kurejesha. Ukikosa kazi, wasiliana na HELB moja kwa moja kupata makubaliano ya mpango wa kulipa. Kupuuza kutaleta adhabu na kuorodheshwa kwa CRB.

Q: Nililipa HELB yangu yote. Je, nahitaji cheti gani?
A: Nenda kwenye portal ya HELB, omba Clearance Certificate. Ni bure. Hutapewa ikiwa bado una malimbikizo.

Q: Mwajiri wangu anakata kwenye mshahara lakini HELB inasema niko nyuma. Nifanyeje?
A: Kusanya risiti za mshahara zinazoonyesha makato. Peleka taarifa kwa HELB na umwombe mwajiri wako afanye marekebisho. Hili ni jambo la kawaida.

Q: Niliomba HELB lakini pesa haijafika. Nifanye nini?
A: Angalia portal yako. Ikiwa inasema "disbursed", fuatilia benki yako na chuo. Ikiwa bado "pending" kwa muda mrefu, piga HELB **0711 052 000** au nenda ofisi ya karibu.

### English
Q: What is the difference between a scholarship and a HELB loan?
A: A scholarship is free money you do not repay. A HELB loan must be repaid with interest after you complete your studies. The new funding model gives you a combination of both.

Q: Can I apply for HELB without a KRA PIN?
A: You can start with your KCSE index number if you are under 18, but a KRA PIN is strongly recommended for both you and your parents. Without it, your application may be incomplete, and you may face difficulties later with compliance certificates.
