## SERVICE 2: SHA (Social Health Authority)

### What is it
The Social Health Authority (SHA) replaced NHIF on 1 October 2024. It is Kenya's mandatory health insurance system under Universal Health Coverage. Every person residing in Kenya must register.

SHA manages three funds:
- Primary Healthcare Fund (PHCF) – preventive and basic care at Level 2 and 3 facilities (dispensaries, health centres), free for all registered Kenyans
- Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF) – specialized inpatient, outpatient, maternity, surgery, and chronic disease care at Level 4 to 6 facilities
- Emergency Chronic and Critical Illness Fund (ECCIF) – emergency, critical care, and catastrophic expenses beyond SHIF limits

### Quick Facts
- **Registration fee**: FREE
- **Register via**: *147# (USSD), sha.go.ke (online), or any Huduma Centre / SHA branch
- **Need**: National ID, active phone number, digital passport photo (online); printed photos (physical)
- **Coverage starts**: immediately, no waiting period
- **Payment for employed**: 2.75% of gross salary (employee) + 2.75% (employer), minimum KES 300/month
- **Payment for self‑employed / informal**: 2.75% of declared annual income, minimum KES 300/month
- **Payment for unemployed / indigent**: fully covered by government
- **NHIF**: completely replaced by SHA; old NHIF cards and numbers are no longer valid

## PART A: WHO MUST REGISTER

Everyone residing in Kenya must register, including:
- All Kenyan citizens regardless of age or income
- Self‑employed and informal sector workers (traders, boda boda riders, salon owners, matatu operators, farmers, hawkers)
- Students in higher education institutions
- Persons with disabilities, elderly, orphans
- Unemployed and indigent persons (government pays their contributions)
- Legal residents with valid work permits
- Refugees with valid refugee ID

Registration is free. No fee charged.

Document needs depend on registration method. Read carefully.

### FOR PHYSICAL HUDUMA CENTRE REGISTRATION
- Original National ID and photocopies

- Phone number in your name
- KRA PIN if employed or self‑employed
- Family documents if adding dependants

### FOR ADDING DEPENDANTS (all methods)

**Spouse:**
- Marriage certificate, OR statutory affidavit, OR Chief's letter confirming marriage
- Spouse's active phone number (OTP will be sent for consent)

**Biological child UNDER 18:**
- Birth certificate number, OR birth notification reference number (for infants under 6 months only)

**Biological child 18 to 20 years:**
- Their National ID number only (no school letter needed)

**Biological child 21 to 25 years IN SCHOOL:**
- Their National ID number

**Child with disability ANY age:**
- National ID (if 18+)
- NCPWD disability card or certificate
- Can remain as a dependant beyond 25 if disability is confirmed

**Adopted child:**
- Court adoption order
- Birth certificate
- If over 21 and in school, add school proof

**Child under guardianship:**
- Court guardianship order
- Birth certificate
- Same age rules as biological children apply

**Non‑citizens:**
- Alien ID or Refugee ID

## PART C: HOW TO REGISTER

SHA offers three official registration channels.

### METHOD 1 – USSD *147# (fastest, no internet)
Works on Safaricom, Airtel, and Telkom. No smartphone or data needed. No passport photo needed. Best for quick registration anywhere.

**Step by step:**
1. Dial *147# on your phone
2. Select Social Health Authority from menu
3. Choose Option 1 for Kenyan citizen, or Option 2 for foreign resident
4. Accept terms and conditions
5. Enter your National ID number exactly
6. System verifies your name via IPRS database
7. Set a secure 4‑digit PIN – keep this PIN private, do not share it
8. Declare employment status: 1 – Employed, 2 – Self‑employed, 3 – Unemployed
9. Declare civil status: 1 – Single, 2 – Married, 3 – Divorced, 4 – Widow, 6 – Widower
10. Declare if Person With Disability (PWD)
11. Select county and sub‑county of residence
12. Complete household details
13. Receive welcome SMS confirming registration with your SHA Member ID

**IMPORTANT:** You can register yourself via USSD, but to add dependants (spouse and children) you must log into the sha.go.ke online portal or visit a Huduma Centre. Dependants cannot be fully added via USSD alone.

### METHOD 2 – Online via sha.go.ke or afyayangu.go.ke
Best for full registration with dependants and document uploads. Requires a smartphone or computer with internet.

**Step by step:**
1. Go to sha.go.ke and click Register
2. Agree to terms and conditions
3. Enter your National ID and phone number
4. Enter the OTP sent to your phone via SMS
5. Create a secure 4‑digit PIN
6. Create a digital signature as prompted
7. Upload a recent passport photo (digital JPEG or PNG, under 2MB)
8. Upload a copy of your National ID
9. Complete personal details: county, sub‑county, employment status, income details
10. Add dependants if registering a family (follow document requirements in Part B)
11. Complete the Means Test if self‑employed or unemployed – this determines your contribution amount
12. Make your first payment via M‑Pesa: Paybill **200222**, Account number: your National ID number
13. Receive confirmation SMS and email

### METHOD 3 – Physical at Huduma Centre or SHA branch
Best for those without internet or who need in‑person help. Bring printed passport photos.

**What to bring:**
- Original National ID and photocopies
- 2 recent printed passport photos
- Phone number in your name
- KRA PIN if employed or self‑employed
- Spouse's ID if registering a family
- Children's birth certificates if applicable

**Step by step:**
1. Visit the nearest Huduma Centre or SHA branch
2. Officer captures your details in the SHA system
3. Biometric data (fingerprints) captured
4. Officer helps you set up a 4‑digit PIN
5. Add dependants with the officer's assistance
6. Receive confirmation and your SHA Member ID

**Special arrangements** are available for persons with disabilities, elderly persons, marginalized communities, persons under lawful custody, and persons incapacitated by illness.

Community Health Promoters (CHPs) also conduct mass registration in communities. Ask locally if a CHP registration exercise is happening in your area – no need to travel far.

## PART D: HOW TO ADD DEPENDANTS

One SHA contribution covers your entire household. You do NOT pay extra to add dependants.

**CRITICAL – what most people get wrong:**  
Adding dependants must be done via the sha.go.ke online portal or at a Huduma Centre. You CANNOT fully add dependants via USSD *147# alone.

### Adding your SPOUSE online
1. Log into sha.go.ke with your ID and PIN
2. Go to My Profile → Dependants tab
3. Click Add Dependant → select Spouse
4. Enter spouse's full name, ID number, date of birth, and contact information
5. Upload one proof of relationship: marriage certificate, statutory affidavit, or Chief's letter confirming marriage
6. Enter the spouse's active phone number
7. The system sends an OTP to the spouse's phone
8. The spouse must enter the OTP to confirm consent – OTP expires in **5 minutes**
9. Click Save – the request enters a verification queue
10. Verification can take up to **7 business days**

### Adding CHILDREN – age rules

**Child under 18:**
- Enter birth certificate number OR birth notification reference (under 6 months)
- No ID required

**Child 18 to 20 years:**
- Enter their National ID number only
- No school letter required at this age

**Child 21 to 25 years AND in school:**
- Enter their National ID number
- Upload proof of school enrollment: student ID, registrar letter, or fees statement
- Coverage ends at age 25 or when they leave school, whichever comes first

**Child over 25 NOT disabled:**
- Cannot be added as a dependant
- Must register as an independent SHA member

**Child with disability any age:**
- National ID if 18+
- NCPWD disability card or certificate
- Can remain on your cover beyond age 25

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** You do NOT need to visit a Huduma Centre physically to add dependants. This can be done on sha.go.ke from your phone at home.

## PART E: CONTRIBUTION AMOUNTS

### EMPLOYED (formal sector)
- Employee pays **2.75%** of gross monthly salary
- Employer also pays **2.75%** of gross monthly salary
- Total contribution = **5.5%** of gross salary
- Minimum monthly payment: **KES 300** regardless of salary

**Contribution examples:**
- Salary KES 6,000 → employee pays KES 165 (minimum applies, so KES 300 total)
- Salary KES 12,000 → employee pays KES 330
- Salary KES 30,000 → employee pays KES 825
- Salary KES 50,000 → employee pays KES 1,375
- Salary KES 100,000 → employee pays KES 2,750
- Salary KES 200,000 → employee pays KES 5,500

There is no upper cap – a higher salary means a higher payment.

Employers must remit contributions by the **9th of the following month**. SHA contributions qualify for KRA insurance relief at **15%**, up to **KES 5,000** per month.

**Employer who deducts but fails to remit:** faces a fine of up to **KES 2 million**, OR **3 years imprisonment**, OR both. Report to SHA or KRA immediately.

### SELF-EMPLOYED / INFORMAL SECTOR
- Rate: **2.75%** of declared annual household income
- Minimum monthly: **KES 300**
- No maximum cap
- Average range: **KES 300 to KES 880** per month (depends on means test outcome)

Complete the Means Test tool on sha.go.ke or Afya Yangu to get your exact amount.

**Pay via M‑Pesa:** Paybill **200222**, Account number: your National ID number.

**LIPA SHA POLE POLE – flexible payment:** Pay daily, weekly, or monthly instalments towards the annual contribution. Available through the Hustler Fund integration. Ideal for boda boda riders, mama mbogas, casual labourers, and hawkers.

### UNEMPLOYED / INDIGENT
The government pays your SHA contributions fully. You still MUST register – just don't pay.

**Automatically eligible if you are:**
- Registered under the Inua Jamii cash transfer programme
- An orphan or vulnerable child in government care
- A person with a severe disability unable to work
- An elderly person without income or family

**Also eligible if identified by:**
- Community Health Promoters during mass registration exercises
- County government social development offices
- Health facility waiver records

Confirm indigent status at any SHA office or Huduma Centre. Sponsors can check eligible households at sponsor.sha.go.ke.

## PART F: HOW TO USE SHA AT HOSPITAL

SHA coverage is activated immediately on registration. There is no waiting period.

You can use SHA at any accredited public, private, or faith‑based facility. Check if a hospital is accredited at sha.go.ke by searching the facilities list.

**Step by step at the hospital:**
1. Before going – check that your status is **Active**: dial *147# or go to eligibility.sha.go.ke
2. Bring the National ID you used during SHA registration
3. Present your ID at the hospital reception or insurance desk
4. Hospital staff initiate your visit in the SHA system
5. You receive a confirmation number for the visit
6. Verify that your SHIF status is **Active**
7. If SHIF is inactive, activate it immediately, then return to the health desk for a new reference number BEFORE admission proceeds

**CRITICAL:** Failure to complete these steps at the time of admission may result in SHA denying coverage for inpatient costs.

Public Level 2 to 4 facilities should NOT charge you out of pocket. A zero co‑payment policy is now in effect. If you are charged illegally, report it to SHA immediately.

### Services covered

**Outpatient:**
- Unlimited visits at Level 2 to 4 facilities
- Consultations, diagnostics, prescriptions

**Inpatient:**
- Up to **180 days**
- Nursing, meals, lab tests, imaging, medications, post‑discharge care

**Maternity:**
- Antenatal visits
- Normal delivery
- Caesarean section (up to **KES 30,000**)
- Essential newborn services

**Emergency:**
- Free treatment for the first **24 hours**
- Accident and evacuation services

**Chronic disease:**
- Diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and other long‑term conditions

**Specialized services:**
- Chemotherapy: up to **KES 800,000**
- Renal dialysis: **2 sessions per week, 8 sessions per month**
- ICU care: **KES 28,000** per day, maximum **12 days**
- CT scans, MRI, PET scans
- Mental health inpatient: up to **180 days**
- Rehabilitation: up to **KES 67,200**

**Overseas treatment:**
- Up to **KES 500,000** for eligible cases

**Last Expense Benefit:**
- **KES 300,000** paid to the family upon the death of a principal SHA member (activated 2026, paid within **48 hours**)

**Assistive devices:**
- Hearing aids, crutches, walking frames, therapeutic footwear for eligible members

No exclusions for pre‑existing conditions. No age limit for eligibility.

### Method 5 – Eligibility checker
Go to eligibility.sha.go.ke. Enter your ID number and click Check – it shows **Active** or **Inactive**. Use this before visiting a hospital.

### Understanding your status
- **ACTIVE** – payments up to date, full access to all SHA‑accredited services
- **PENDING** – still being processed; means testing may be required
- **INACTIVE** – missed payments or not registered; coverage suspended until you pay again
- **NOT FOUND** – not in the system; register immediately via *147# or sha.go.ke

## PART H: SHA vs OLD NHIF

NHIF is completely gone. SHA replaced it on **1 October 2024**. Old NHIF cards are no longer valid. ALL former NHIF members must re‑register with SHA. Your NHIF number does NOT transfer.

**Key differences:**
- NHIF used fixed bands (maximum KES 1,700); SHA uses **2.75% of salary with no upper cap**
- NHIF had a waiting period before coverage; SHA coverage **starts immediately**
- NHIF covered mostly inpatient services; SHA covers the **full spectrum, including free primary care**
- NHIF required a physical card; SHA uses **digital ID and biometric verification**
- NHIF offered a limited subsidy for the poor; SHA **fully funds indigent households**
- NHIF had some pre‑existing condition exclusions; SHA has **no exclusions whatsoever**
- NHIF applied age restrictions; SHA has **no age limit**

### English
Q: Am a boda boda rider. How do I pay?
A: Register as self‑employed via *147#. Minimum **KES 300** per month. Use LIPA SHA POLE POLE via the Hustler Fund to pay in small daily or weekly amounts. M‑Pesa Paybill **200222**, Account number: your National ID number.

Q: Can I use SHA at private hospitals?
A: Yes, at SHA‑accredited private facilities. Check sha.go.ke for the updated accredited list. Not all private hospitals are accredited.

Q: My employer is not remitting contributions. What do I do?
A: Report to SHA or KRA immediately. An employer who deducts but fails to remit faces a fine of up to **KES 2 million** OR **3 years imprisonment** OR both.

Q: Do Kenyans abroad need SHA?
A: No, it is not required if you are living abroad. You can register voluntarily to use Kenyan health services when visiting.

Q: How do I report SHA fraud?
A: Email fraud@sha.go.ke or call **0800 720 601**. Do not engage with unofficial SHA agents or click unknown registration links.

Q: What is the difference between SHIF and SHA?
A: SHA is the authority – the organisation. SHIF (Social Health Insurance Fund) is one of the three funds SHA manages. When a hospital checks whether you can access specialist care, they check your SHIF status.

## PART K: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Using a nickname instead of your full name as it appears on your National ID
- Entering the wrong KRA PIN during registration
- Registering twice using different numbers (creates duplicate accounts)
- Skipping dependent registration – your family is NOT automatically covered; they may be turned away at the hospital
- Going to the hospital without checking your status first
- Thinking your old NHIF card still works – it does NOT
- Using fake or unofficial SHA registration sites or agents
