## SERVICE 3: KRA PIN

### What is it
KRA PIN is a Personal Identification Number issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority. You need it for employment, business, rental income, HELB loans, land, vehicles, bank accounts, and most government transactions.

### Quick Facts
- **Official fee**: FREE
- **Apply at**: itax.kra.go.ke (iTax) – the main portal; also available on ecitizen.kra.go.ke
- **Need**: National ID, active phone number, accessible email address, income source details
- **PIN issued**: instantly online – a copy is also sent to your email
- **PIN Without Obligation (PWO)**: available for students, diplomats, and others with no taxable income
- **Check first**: many people already have a PIN – use itax.kra.go.ke/KRA-Portal/pinChecker.htm before applying

---

## PART A: DO YOU NEED ONE AND WHICH TYPE

### Who must have a KRA PIN
- Employed persons
- Business owners
- Anyone with rental income
- Anyone applying for HELB
- Anyone doing land, vehicle, or bank transactions
- Anyone signing a government contract

### PIN Without Obligation (PWO)
For people with no taxable income: students, diplomats, housewives, etc.  
It lets you do transactions requiring a PIN **without needing to file tax returns**.  
You **cannot** get a Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) or use eTIMS until you update your obligations.

Most students end up needing a PIN because HELB, universities, and banks all demand one.  
Apply for **PWO** so you are not required to file annual returns.

### Check if you already have a PIN
Many people were registered years ago by an employer or agent without knowing.  
Check first before applying:  
https://itax.kra.go.ke/KRA-Portal/pinChecker.htm

If you find one exists – **do NOT apply again**. Retrieve it instead (see Part E).

---

## PART B: DOCUMENTS AND REQUIREMENTS

You do NOT need to go anywhere in person. Everything is done online.

**What you need before starting:**
- Valid National ID (you'll enter the number)
- Active mobile phone number (OTP will be sent here)
- Email address you can access (PIN certificate is sent here)
- Details of your income source (employer name, business type, etc.)

**IMPORTANT:** If you use a cyber café, make sure they use **YOUR phone number and YOUR email** – not theirs. If they use their own details, you will be permanently locked out of your own PIN account.

---

## PART C: HOW TO APPLY

### Method 1 – iTax online (recommended)
The main and most reliable portal. Use **Google Chrome or Firefox** on a desktop. Mobile browsers often fail at CAPTCHA or document download. Turn off pop‑up blockers before starting.

**Step by step:**
1. Go to https://itax.kra.go.ke
2. Click **New PIN Registration**
3. Select **Individual**, then **Online Form**
4. Enter your National ID number and date of birth – the system auto‑fills some details
5. Fill Section A – basic personal info, Section B – your tax obligations, Section C – your income source
6. Enter your physical address, active phone number, and email
7. Verify your email with the OTP sent
8. State the reason for application
9. Accept the privacy terms
10. Upload any required documents
11. Submit
12. PIN certificate is **issued instantly** and sent to your email – save it

### Method 2 – eCitizen
KRA services are also available on eCitizen at https://ecitizen.kra.go.ke (includes PIN registration and nil returns). Official guides still mainly point to iTax. Either works – pick one and stick to it. **Do not** create profiles on both.

### Method 3 – Mobile USSD
Dial ***572#** on any phone (including basic feature phones). Follow the menu prompts for PIN Registration. Good option if you have no internet access.

### Method 4 – KRA M‑Service App
Download from Google Play or Apple App Store. Offers full PIN registration and other services.

### Method 5 – Huduma Centre
Walk into any Huduma Centre near you. Counter service is **free**. The cyber café inside or nearby charges **KES 150–200** facilitation fee for form filling.  
**Bring:** Original National ID, active phone number, email address, income source details.

---

## PART D: COSTS

KRA PIN registration is **FREE**.

You only pay if you use outside help:
- Cyber café facilitation: **KES 150–200**
- Huduma Centre cyber: standard facility fee
- Agent for complex cases: varies

Any person demanding cash payment for the PIN itself is lying. The PIN is free.

## PART E: COMMON PROBLEMS AND FIXES

### "ID already registered" error
Means a KRA PIN already exists under your ID. **Do NOT apply again.** Retrieve the existing one:
- Try **Forgot Password** on iTax using the registered email (if you know it)
- Email **callcentre@kra.go.ke** with a scanned copy of your ID (both sides)
- Call **0711 099 999** and follow the PIN retrieval prompts
Many people pay a cyber **KES 150–200** to help with PIN retrieval and password reset.

### PIN is inactive or locked
Password blocks auto‑reset after **24 hours**.  
If still locked:
- Email **callcentre@kra.go.ke** or **DTDOnlineSupport@kra.go.ke** with your PIN and ID
- Visit a Huduma Centre or KRA office
- You may need to file **nil returns** for missing years – do this free on eCitizen or iTax

### Name is misspelled on PIN certificate
Log into iTax → Registration → **Amend PIN Details** → update and save.  
Or email **callcentre@kra.go.ke** with your PIN and a colour scan of your ID.  
If you have a passport (not ID), you must visit a KRA office or Huduma Centre – online name amendment is not available for non‑residents.

### Lost your PIN certificate
Log into iTax → Registration tab → select **Reprint PIN Certificate**.  
A copy is also in your registered email.  
If locked out, email **callcentre@kra.go.ke** with your ID scan.

---

## PART F: AFTER YOU GET YOUR PIN

### If you got a PWO and are now employed
Update your PIN to add employment obligations:  
Log into iTax → Amend PIN Details → add **Income Tax Individual** and **PAYE** obligations.  
Your employer also needs your PIN to register you for tax deduction.  
Once obligations are added, you **must** start filing annual returns.

### If you got married or changed your name
Log into iTax → Registration → Amend PIN Details → update name, address, and contact details.  
Attach your marriage certificate or updated ID.

### If you have not filed returns for years
Your PIN may be flagged as inactive. Check status at the PIN Checker tool first.  
File all overdue returns (even nil returns) to reactivate – you can do this free on eCitizen or the M‑Service app. Clear any penalties that have accumulated.

### About eTIMS (businesses only)
From January 2026, KRA validates business income and expenses against eTIMS invoices. Your PIN must be linked to your invoices. If you are not in business, this does not affect your PIN.

## PART G: COMMON QUESTIONS

### Swahili
Q: PIN ya KRA ni bure au inalipwa?
A: Bure kabisa. Usajili kwenye iTax haufai chochote. Ukitumia kibanda cha mtandao wanaweza kutoza **KES 150–200** kwa msaada wa kujaza fomu – lakini PIN yenyewe ni bure.

Q: Sijawahi kuwa na PIN. Nianze wapi?
A: Kwanza angalia kama tayari una PIN: https://itax.kra.go.ke/KRA-Portal/pinChecker.htm  
Kama huna, nenda itax.kra.go.ke, bonyeza New PIN Registration na ufuate hatua. PIN hutolewa papo hapo mtandaoni.

Q: Mimi ni mwanafunzi. Ninahitaji PIN?
A: Ndiyo uwezekano mkubwa – HELB, vyuo, na benki wote wanahitaji PIN ya KRA. Omba **PWO** (PIN Bila Wajibu) ili usiwe na wajibu wa kuwasilisha marejesho ya kodi.

Q: Ninaweza kuomba kwa simu yangu?
A: Ndiyo. Piga ***572#** kwenye simu yoyote. Au pakua app ya KRA M‑Service kutoka Google Play au Apple App Store. Kwa utegemezi zaidi tumia Chrome au Firefox kwenye kompyuta ya mezani.

Q: Cyber aliandikisha PIN kwa barua pepe yake. Sasa nifanye nini?
A: Wasiliana nao upate barua pepe na nenosiri. Kisha ingia iTax, nenda Registration, ubadilishe barua pepe na namba ya simu iwe yako wewe. Ikiwa hawashirikiani, wasiliana na KRA kwa callcentre@kra.go.ke au piga **0711 099 999**.

Q: Sijawasilisha marejesho kwa miaka mingi. Je, PIN yangu bado inafanya kazi?
A: Inaweza kuwa imezimwa. Angalia kwenye PIN Checker kwanza. Kisha wasilisha marejesho yote yaliyochelewa – hata kama ni sifuri – kwenye eCitizen au M‑Service app. Hii ni bure na inaweza kuiamsha PIN yako tena.

Q: Nini kitatokea nikifanya shughuli bila PIN?
A: Huwezi kukamilisha shughuli hiyo kisheria. Huwezi kusajili ardhi, kutoa leseni ya gari, kufungua akaunti ya benki, au kusaini mkataba wa serikali bila PIN ya KRA. Kuna pia adhabu ya **KES 2,000** kwa kukiuka sheria, ingawa haitumiki mara nyingi mwanzo.

Q: Ninabadilishaje PIN yangu kutoka PWO hadi ajira baada ya kupata kazi?
A: Ingia iTax – Amend PIN Details – ongeza wajibu wa Income Tax Individual na PAYE. Mwajiri wako pia atahitaji PIN yako kukuandikisha kwa makato ya kodi. Baada ya hapo lazima uwasilishe marejesho kila mwaka.

### English
Q: Is KRA PIN free?
A: Yes, completely free. Registration on iTax costs nothing. If you use a cyber café they may charge **KES 150–200** for assistance – but the PIN itself is free.

Q: I've never had a PIN. Where do I start?
A: First check if you already have one: https://itax.kra.go.ke/KRA-Portal/pinChecker.htm. If not, go to itax.kra.go.ke, click New PIN Registration, and follow the steps. The PIN is issued instantly online.

Q: I'm a student. Do I need a PIN?
A: Most likely yes – HELB, universities, and banks all require a KRA PIN. Apply for a **PWO** (PIN Without Obligation) so you don't have to file tax returns.

Q: Can I apply on my phone?
A: Yes. Dial ***572#** on any phone, or download the KRA M‑Service app from Google Play or the Apple App Store. For the most reliable experience, use Chrome or Firefox on a desktop computer.

Q: A cyber café used their own email for my PIN. What now?
A: Get the email and password from them. Then log into iTax, go to Registration, and change the email and phone number to your own. If they won't cooperate, contact KRA at callcentre@kra.go.ke or call **0711 099 999**.

Q: I haven't filed returns for years. Is my PIN still active?
A: It may be flagged as inactive. Check at the PIN Checker first. Then file all overdue returns – even nil returns – on eCitizen or the M‑Service app. This is free and can reactivate your PIN.

Q: What happens if I do a transaction without a PIN?
A: You cannot complete the transaction legally. You cannot register land, license a vehicle, open a bank account, or sign a government contract without a KRA PIN. There is also a **KES 2,000** penalty under the law, though it is rarely enforced on first contact.

Q: How do I switch from a PWO to an employment PIN?
A: Log into iTax → Amend PIN Details → add Income Tax Individual and PAYE obligations. Your employer also needs your PIN to register you for tax deduction. After that, you must file annual returns.
