## SERVICE 4: PASSPORT (Kenyan e-Passport)

### What is it
The Kenyan e-Passport is a biometric travel document with an electronic chip containing your photo, fingerprints, and personal data. All current Kenyan passports are e-Passports. They are dark blue, machine-readable, and valid for 10 years. Mandatory for international travel outside the East African Community.

### Quick Facts
- **Official fee**: Ordinary A (34 pages) – **KES 7,550**; Ordinary B (50 pages) – **KES 9,550**; Ordinary C (66 pages) – **KES 12,050**
- **Apply at**: ecitizen.go.ke → Department of Immigration Services
- **Need**: National ID, birth certificate, 3 passport-size photos, parents' ID copies, recommender (known, non-relative, answerable by phone)
- **Biometrics**: in person at one of 9 centres; bring original documents even if uploaded
- **Processing**: at least 10 working days (official); often 3–6 weeks in reality
- **Collection**: same centre where biometrics were captured; after SMS or eCitizen notification
- **Lost/stolen replacement fee**: **KES 20,050**

## PART A: PASSPORT TYPES AND FEES

### Ordinary A Series – 34 pages
Most citizens use this one.  
Fee: **KES 7,550**. Valid 10 years.

### Ordinary B Series – 50 pages
For frequent travellers needing more pages.  
Fee: **KES 9,550**. Valid 10 years.

### Ordinary C Series – 66 pages
Maximum pages available.  
Fee: **KES 12,050**. Valid 10 years.

### East African Temporary Permit
Travel within East Africa only. Valid 1 year.  
Fee: **KES 1,050**.

### Interstate Pass (EAC)
Free. For travel to Uganda and Rwanda only.

### Who can apply
Kenyan citizen by birth or registration.  
Must have a valid National ID.  
Names on ID and birth certificate **must match**.  
If they don't match – fix this first before applying. Get an affidavit or correct at Civil Registration. Coming with mismatched documents will get your application rejected.

---

## PART B: DOCUMENTS NEEDED

Bring originals AND photocopies of everything.  
No proxies. You **MUST** appear in person.

**For a first-time adult applicant:**

**APPLICATION FORM:**
- Filled Form 19 – printed from eCitizen
- 3 printed payment invoices/receipts

**IDENTITY DOCUMENTS:**
- Original National ID + clear photocopy
- Original Birth Certificate + photocopy

**PHOTOS:**
- 3 current passport-size photos (bring even if biometric photo is taken onsite – some centres still ask for them)

**PARENTS' DOCUMENTS:**
- Copies of both parents' National IDs
- If a parent is deceased – their death certificate instead of ID

**IF NAMES DON'T MATCH:**
- Affidavit or deed poll explaining the difference
- Marriage certificate if name changed after marriage

**RECOMMENDER:**
- **Official rule:** recommender's ID copy is **no longer required**
- **Reality:** officers still call your recommender during biometrics. Pick a Kenyan citizen who is **NOT a relative** and who **will answer their phone** that day. Have them on standby. If they don't pick up, your file may be held.

*Source: immigration.go.ke/application-requirements*

## PART C: HOW TO APPLY

All applications MUST start online via eCitizen.  
No walk-ins without eCitizen pre-application.

### Step by step process
1. Register or log in at ecitizen.go.ke
2. Go to Department of Immigration Services, or visit immigration.ecitizen.go.ke
3. Select **Passport Application** – read instructions carefully
4. Choose your passport type (A, B, or C)
5. Fill Form 19 – use the exact name as on your ID and birth certificate. Enter parent details exactly as on their documents. Enter recommender name and phone number.
6. Upload scans of your ID and birth certificate
7. Select **M-Pesa** as the payment method (cards often fail)
8. Pay the exact fee – do not close the browser until payment confirms
9. **If payment shows "pending":** wait 30 to 60 minutes, refresh, log out and back in. DO NOT pay again. Paying twice is very hard to reverse.
10. Download and print: the completed Form 19, and all 3 payment receipts/invoices
11. Book an appointment at your chosen Immigration centre via the eCitizen portal

### Booking appointment – real tips
Nairobi (Nyayo House) slots fill up fast – they can be full 2 to 4 weeks ahead.  
Check early morning or late at night – new slots sometimes appear then.  
Regional centres like Eldoret, Kisumu, Embu, and Kisii often have earlier slots, but you will need to travel.

## PART D: BIOMETRICS APPOINTMENT

### Nine centres available
- Nairobi – Nyayo House, Ground Floor
- Mombasa – Uhuru na Kazi
- Kisumu – New PC's Office
- Embu
- Nakuru
- Kisii
- Eldoret – Public Works
- Kericho – Ardhi House
- Bungoma

### What to bring on appointment day
- Printed Form 19 + all 3 receipts
- Original National ID + photocopy
- Original Birth Certificate + photocopy
- 3 passport-size photos
- Copies of parents' IDs or death certificates
- Affidavit or deed poll if names differ
- Recommender reachable by phone

### What happens there
- Officer checks and verifies your documents
- Digital photo is taken onsite
- Fingerprints scanned
- Officer may call your recommender on the spot
- You receive an acknowledgment slip and token for tracking – keep this safely

### Dress code for biometrics – THIS MATTERS
- **Women:** avoid white tops, shiny fabric, heavy makeup, and flashy jewellery.
- **Men:** plain non-white shirt, no oily or shiny skin.
- **Henna:** thick henna can block fingerprint scan – time your appointment accordingly.
- If your photo is rejected onsite, you may be sent to a nearby photo kiosk and must re-queue.
- Hijabs and cultural headgear are generally allowed – declare at the desk.

## PART E: FEES AND REAL COSTS

### Official fees (paid via eCitizen only)
- Ordinary A – 34 pages: **KES 7,550**
- Ordinary B – 50 pages: **KES 9,550**
- Ordinary C – 66 pages: **KES 12,050**
- Lost or mutilated replacement: **KES 20,050**
- Temporary Permit: **KES 1,050**
- Interstate Pass: Free

### Real-world extra costs
- Cyber café printing and scanning: **KES 100–300**
- Passport photos at a studio: **KES 150–300**
- Affidavit: **KES 200–1,000**
- Deed poll for name change: **KES 1,500–3,000+**
- Transport and food – especially at Nyayo House – can equal or exceed the official fee

**IMPORTANT:** The government fee is fixed and paid online only. Any cash demand at the centre beyond your eCitizen receipt is unofficial. Report to complaints@immigration.go.ke.

*Source: immigration.go.ke/type-and-fees*

## PART F: TRACKING YOUR APPLICATION

### Method 1 – eCitizen
Log in, check under **My Applications**.

### Method 2 – SMS
Send your application number to **22222**.

### Processing times
- **Official:** 10 working days for a first-time application. Service Charter 2025: 14 days standard.
- **Reality:** most people wait **3 to 6 weeks**. Sometimes longer during backlogs.
- **DO NOT** book any travel until you are holding the physical passport in your hand.

## PART G: COLLECTION

Once you receive notification via eCitizen or SMS:

**Go to the SAME centre where you did your biometrics.**  
You cannot collect from a different centre.

**Bring:**
- Invoice with your tracking token
- Original National ID
- Old passport (if you were replacing one)

### Third-party collection
Not clearly defined officially. Contact your specific centre directly.  
Likely requirements:
- Signed authorization letter from the applicant
- Copy of the applicant's ID and token
- Collector's original ID

## PART H: SPECIAL SITUATIONS

### If your passport is lost or stolen
1. Report to the nearest police station
2. Get a police abstract
3. Swear an affidavit explaining what happened
4. Fill Form 19 online – pay **KES 20,050**
5. Bring your ID, police abstract, affidavit, and a copy of the lost passport's bio-data page (if you have it)
6. Book an appointment and attend biometrics

### If your name changed – marriage or deed poll
Apply for a replacement. Bring:
- Marriage certificate or deed poll + Kenya Gazette notice
- Written explanation addressed to the Passport Control Officer
- Pay the replacement fee. Attend biometrics again.

### If your passport expired
Same process as a first-time application.  
Bring your old passport instead of your birth certificate.  
Pay the fee for the passport type you choose.

### If there is an error on an issued passport
Contact customer care at the centre where it was issued. Likely requires a new application and fee.

### If your application is delayed or rejected
Visit the customer care desk at your centre.  
Name mismatches are the **number one cause** of rejection – fix with an affidavit or correct at Civil Registration before applying.

## PART I: DIASPORA APPLICATIONS

Apply via eCitizen, then attend an appointment at the nearest Kenyan embassy: London, Washington DC, Berlin, Pretoria, Dubai, or Paris.

**Notes:**
- Appointment slots at embassies are very limited. Apply well in advance.
- May require notarized copies, money orders, and a prepaid courier for delivery.
- The passport is printed in Nairobi and shipped – this adds extra time.
- Total time from application to receiving your passport abroad: commonly **2 to 4 months**.
- Check your specific embassy website for exact local requirements.

Email: overseaspassports@immigration.go.ke

## PART J: COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Paying twice when eCitizen shows "pending" – wait, refresh, and confirm before paying again
- Booking flights or travel before holding the actual physical passport
- Using a relative as recommender – pick a non-relative who will answer their phone
- Wearing white or shiny clothes to biometrics
- Not bringing original documents – scans uploaded online do NOT replace originals on appointment day. You will be turned away.
- Trying to fix name mismatches at the counter instead of sorting it out beforehand
- Going to a different centre for collection

## PART K: COMMON QUESTIONS

### Swahili
Q: Passport ya Kenya inagharimu ngapi?
A: Kijitabu cha kurasa 34 ni **KES 7,550**. Kurasa 50 ni **KES 9,550**. Kurasa 66 ni **KES 12,050**. Ada zote hulipwa mtandaoni kupitia eCitizen tu. Ulipie M-Pesa kwa usalama zaidi.

Q: Nimelipa lakini inaonyesha "pending". Nilipe tena?
A: Hapana. Subiri dakika 30 hadi 60, kisha refresh na uingie tena. Usilipe mara mbili – kurudisha pesa ni ngumu sana. M-Pesa ndiyo chaguo salama zaidi.

Q: Je, ni lazima niende na mdhamini wangu?
A: Huhitaji nakala ya kitambulisho chake tena. Lakini maafisa bado wanaweza kumpigia simu wakati wa biometrics. Mhakikishe anaweza kujibu simu siku hiyo. Asipopokea, kesi yako inaweza kushikiliwa.

Q: Jina langu kwenye ID ni tofauti na cheti cha kuzaliwa. Nifanye nini?
A: Rekebisha kabla ya kuomba passport. Pata affidavit au fanya marekebisho kwa Civil Registration kwanza. Kuja na nyaraka zisizooana kutasababisha ombi lako likataliwe.

Q: Ninahitaji passport haraka. Kuna express?
A: Rasmi, hakuna express service. Usikubali kulipa mtu nje ya eCitizen kwa "express" – ni hatari. Unaweza kupoteza pesa au kusababisha kesi yako kuzuiliwa. Panga safari baada ya kupokea passport mkononi.

Q: Nafasi za Nyayo House zimejaa. Nifanye nini?
A: Angalia eCitizen mapema asubuhi au usiku wa manane – nafasi mpya zinaweza kuonekana. Au chagua kituo kingine kama Eldoret au Kisumu, ingawa utahitaji usafiri.

Q: Ninaweza kukusanya passport yangu mahali pengine si pale nilipofanya biometrics?
A: Hapana. Lazima urudi kituo kile kile ulichofanya biometrics. Usiende mahali pengine.

Q: Passport yangu imepotea. Nifanye nini?
A: Ripoti polisi kwanza upate police abstract. Kisha apisha affidavit, jaza Form 19 mtandaoni, lipa **KES 20,050**, na uhudhurie biometrics tena.

Q: Nilivaa nguo nyeupe kwa biometrics na picha ilikataliwa. Sasa nifanye nini?
A: Baadhi ya vituo vitakutuma kwa kiosk ya karibu kupiga picha mpya kisha urudi kwenye foleni. Epuka nguo nyeupe, zinazong'aa, au mapambo mengi usoni kabla ya kuenda.

### English
Q: How much does a Kenyan passport cost?
A: The 34-page booklet is **KES 7,550**. 50 pages is **KES 9,550**. 66 pages is **KES 12,050**. All fees are paid online via eCitizen. Use M-Pesa for the most reliable experience.

Q: My payment is showing "pending". Should I pay again?
A: No. Wait 30 to 60 minutes, refresh, and log back in. Do not pay twice – reversing a double payment is very difficult. M-Pesa is the safest option.

Q: Do I need to bring my recommender with me?
A: You no longer need a copy of their ID, but officers may still call them during biometrics. Make sure your recommender can answer their phone that day. If they miss the call, your file may be held.

Q: My name on my ID is different from my birth certificate. What should I do?
A: Fix this before applying. Get an affidavit or correct the records at Civil Registration first. Mismatched documents will get your application rejected.

Q: I need my passport urgently. Is there an express service?
A: Officially, no. Do not pay anyone outside eCitizen for "express" – it's risky. You could lose your money or have your application blocked. Plan your travel after you receive the physical passport.

Q: Nyayo House appointment slots are fully booked. What can I do?
A: Check eCitizen early in the morning or late at night – new slots may appear. Or choose a different centre like Eldoret or Kisumu, though you will need to travel.

Q: Can I collect my passport from a different centre?
A: No. You must return to the same centre where you did your biometrics. Do not go elsewhere.

Q: My passport is lost. What do I do?
A: Report to the police first and get a police abstract. Then swear an affidavit, fill Form 19 online, pay **KES 20,050**, and attend biometrics again.

Q: I wore a white top for biometrics and my photo was rejected. What now?
A: Some centres will ask you to get new photos at a nearby kiosk and re-queue. Avoid white, shiny clothing, and heavy makeup before your appointment.
