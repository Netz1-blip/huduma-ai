## SERVICE 5: NSSF (National Social Security Fund)

### What is it
The National Social Security Fund is a mandatory retirement savings and social protection scheme for all employed and self‑employed Kenyans aged 18 and above. It was established in 1965 and transformed into a full pension scheme by the NSSF Act No. 45 of 2013. You can only register once in your lifetime – your NSSF number stays with you across all jobs forever.

### Quick Facts
- **Registration fee**: FREE
- **Register via**: employer (formal), selfservice.nssf.or.ke (online), *303# (USSD – Haba Haba), or any NSSF branch
- **Need**: National ID, KRA PIN (for full Tier I & II), employer details (if employed)
- **Current contribution rates**: employee 6% + employer 6% of pensionable earnings; Tier I calculated on KES 8,000, Tier II on earnings up to KES 72,000 (maximum total: KES 8,640/month)
- **M-Pesa Paybill**: 333300 (account number: your NSSF number)
- **Haba Haba (informal sector)**: save from KES 25/day; withdraw 30% after 1 year, full access after 5 years or retirement; maternity benefit available
- **Benefits**: old age pension (age 55), survivors, invalidity, funeral grant, emigration withdrawal
- **Check balance**: selfservice.nssf.or.ke, *303#, NSSF app (Android), or any branch (free statement)

---

## PART A: WHO MUST REGISTER

Everyone aged 18 and above who is:
- Employed in the formal sector (full‑time, part‑time, or contract)
- Self‑employed
- Informal sector worker (bodaboda rider, mama mboga, hawker, farmer, artisan, casual labourer)
- Any person wishing to voluntarily save for retirement

If you are employed, your employer must register you **within 21 days** of hiring you. If they refuse or delay, visit the nearest NSSF branch immediately with your ID and employment details.

---

## PART B: DOCUMENTS NEEDED

### For employed members (registered through employer):
- Original National ID (or valid Passport / Alien ID)
- KRA PIN
- Employer name and physical address
- Bank account details (for future benefit payments)

### For self‑employed / voluntary members:
- National ID
- Active mobile phone number
- KRA PIN is not strictly required for Haba Haba via *303#, but needed for full Tier I & II contributions

### Practical extras to always carry:
- Photocopies of your ID
- Payslips (if you need to prove employer deductions)
- Employment or termination letters (if claiming benefits)
- Marriage certificate, deed poll, or gazette notice (if your name has changed)

---

## PART C: HOW TO REGISTER

### Method 1 – Through your employer (formal sector)
Most common for salaried workers.
1. Give your National ID and KRA PIN to HR or payroll office
2. Employer fills in and submits the registration forms to NSSF
3. You receive your NSSF membership number – keep this number safe, it is yours for life
4. Contributions start automatically from your next payroll
5. Check your first payslip to confirm that deductions are appearing

### Method 2 – Self‑service portal (online)
For self‑employed or voluntary members. Use Chrome or Firefox on a desktop computer.
1. Go to https://selfservice.nssf.or.ke
2. Click **Member Registration**
3. Fill in all personal details accurately
4. Print the application notification
5. Visit the nearest NSSF branch with the notification, your ID, and any other required documents
6. Branch staff verify your details and give you an **NSSF Encoded PIN Key**
7. Return online and activate your account using the PIN Key via the **Member Activation** link

If the portal times out or confuses you, cyber cafés can help for **KES 100–300**.

### Method 3 – USSD *303# (Haba Haba)
Designed for informal and low‑income workers. No documents needed initially.
1. Dial ***303#** on any phone
2. Follow the prompts to register as an NSSF member
3. Start contributing immediately via M‑Pesa Paybill **333300**

Haba Haba allows savings from **KES 25 per day**.
- Withdraw **30%** after 1 year of consistent saving.
- Full access after **5 years** or upon retirement.
- **Maternity benefit**: women who save at least KES 400/month for 4 consecutive months qualify for KES 4,000/month for 4 months (total KES 16,000).
- All Haba Haba savings are tax‑exempt.

### Method 4 – NSSF Mobile App
Download from Google Play Store. Register, view statements, and track contributions. Mainly Android – iOS availability is limited.

### Method 5 – Physical NSSF branch
Walk in with your ID. Staff assist with registration, duplicate cards (replacement cost: **KES 200**), and status queries.

## PART D: CONTRIBUTIONS AND RATES

Current contribution structure (effective February 2025 – Year 3 of phased implementation):

| Tier | Earnings Band | Employee (6%) | Employer (6%) | Total |
|------|--------------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| Tier I | Up to KES 8,000 | KES 480 | KES 480 | KES 960 |
| Tier II | KES 8,001 – KES 72,000 | 6% of amount above KES 8,000 | 6% of amount above KES 8,000 | Up to KES 7,680 |
| **Maximum (Tier I + II)** | – | **KES 4,320** | **KES 4,320** | **KES 8,640** |

- Lower Earnings Limit (LEL): **KES 8,000**
- Upper Earnings Limit (UEL): **KES 72,000**
- If you earn less than KES 8,000, only Tier I applies.
- Tier II may be contracted out to an RBA‑approved private scheme; most workers have both tiers going to NSSF.

**February 2026 update (Year 4):** earnings bands increased further. If your payslip does not reflect the new rates, follow up with HR. Some smaller employers adjust slowly.

**Self‑employed / voluntary members** can contribute flexibly. To build a meaningful pension, consistent monthly contributions closer to the formal sector rates are encouraged.

### How to pay (M‑Pesa)
- **Paybill:** 333300
- **Account number:** your 9‑digit NSSF number (include leading zeros and trailing 'X' where applicable)
- **Amount:** KES 200 – KES 140,000
- Allow up to **48 hours** for the payment to appear in your NSSF statement. Keep your M‑Pesa confirmation message.

---

## PART E: TRACK YOUR CONTRIBUTIONS

Check your statement every 3 to 6 months. Do not wait until retirement. If your employer deducts but does not remit, you only discover this by checking your statement.

**Ways to check:**
- **Portal:** https://selfservice.nssf.or.ke
- **USSD:** dial ***303#** and follow prompts
- **Mobile App:** available on Android
- **Branch:** request a free printed statement of account

NSSF credits interest annually. For FY 2024/25, interest was declared at **17%**.

## PART F: BENEFITS AND HOW TO CLAIM

All benefit applications must be submitted **in person** at an NSSF branch. Forms are available online, but the claim process itself is physical.

### Old age pension (retirement)
- **When:** age 55, or upon ultimate retirement from regular employment.
- **Bring:** certified retirement letter or termination certificate, NSSF membership card, ID, bank details.
- Straightforward cases may process in a few months; missing employer remittances can delay it significantly. Start early.

### Early retirement
- **When:** age 50 and above, fully retired from paid employment.
- **Documents:** similar to old age pension.

### Survivors pension (death of a member)
**Who can claim (in order):** Spouse → Children → Parents → Siblings → Guardian of minor children → Holder of letters of administration.
- **Bring:** death certificate, deceased member's ID copy, claimant's ID, letter from County Administration confirming dependants, deceased's NSSF number.
- Expect requests for extra documents; bring everything you can.

### Invalidity pension
- **When:** certified permanently incapable of working due to physical or mental disability, or age 50+ with partial permanent incapacity.
- **Bring:** medical/treatment reports, termination letter on medical grounds, NSSF card, ID, bank details. NSSF will appoint their own doctor to examine you.

### Funeral grant
- A cash grant to help with burial expenses for a deceased member's family.
- Many Kenyans are unaware this exists. Visit any NSSF branch **immediately** after a member's death to ask about it. The application window is short.

### Withdrawal benefit (emigration)
- **When:** permanently emigrating to a country outside the East African Community.
- **Bring:** visa outside EAC, sworn affidavit declaring permanent immigration (for Kenyan citizens), termination letter, ticket (for non‑citizens).

## PART G: COMMON PROBLEMS AND FIXES

- **Employer deducts but never remits:** Get your NSSF statement. Compare with payslips. If gaps exist, report to the nearest NSSF branch with payslips and ID. NSSF compliance officers will pursue the employer.
- **Forgot your NSSF number:** Visit any NSSF branch with your ID. Replacement membership card costs **KES 200**.
- **Name mismatch on records:** Visit a branch with marriage certificate, deed poll, or gazette notice. Affidavits may also be required.
- **Portal not working:** Clear browser cache, use Chrome or Firefox on a desktop. Alternatively, use USSD ***303#**.
- **M‑Pesa payment not showing:** Wait 48 hours. If still missing, take your M‑Pesa confirmation message to any branch.
- **Benefits delayed or denied:** Gather all documents and request a formal written explanation from the branch. Escalate to the branch manager. External redress: Office of the Ombudsman at **020‑2270000**.

## PART H: COMMON QUESTIONS

### Swahili
Q: Kila mfanyakazi lazima asajiliwe na NSSF?
A: Ndiyo. Kila mtu mwenye umri wa miaka 18 na kuendelea anayefanya kazi rasmi au kujiajiri anapaswa kujiandikisha.

Q: Nimebadilisha kazi. Nambari yangu ya NSSF itabadilika?
A: Hapana. Nambari yako ya NSSF ni ya maisha yote. Mpe mwajiri wako mpya nambari ile ile.

Q: Ninawezaje kuangalia salio langu la NSSF kwa simu?
A: Piga ***303#** na ufuate maelekezo, au tumia NSSF mobile app (Android). Unaweza pia kutumia selfservice.nssf.or.ke kwenye kivinjari.

Q: Mimi ni mama mboga. Nilipe kiasi gani?
A: Hakuna kiwango cha chini kinacholazimishwa. Kupitia Haba Haba unaweza kuanza na **KES 25 kwa siku**. Kadiri unavyoweka zaidi, ndivyo pensheni yako itakavyokuwa kubwa.

Q: Ninaweza kutoa pesa zangu zote kabla ya kustaafu?
A: Kwa ujumla hapana, isipokuwa unahamia nchi nje ya Jumuiya ya Afrika Mashariki. Wanachama wa Haba Haba wanaweza kutoa 30% baada ya mwaka mmoja na zote baada ya miaka mitano.

Q: NSSF inatoa msaada wa mazishi?
A: Ndiyo. Kuna ruzuku ya mazishi kwa jamaa wa mwanachama aliyefariki. Watu wengi hawaijui. Nenda ofisi ya NSSF mara moja baada ya msiba kuulizia. Muda wa kuomba ni mfupi.

### English
Q: My employer deducts from my salary but I see nothing in my NSSF statement.
A: Get your NSSF statement immediately and compare it to your payslips. If there are gaps, go to the nearest NSSF branch and report it. Bring payslips and your ID.

Q: I just got my first job. How do I make sure NSSF is set up correctly?
A: Give your ID and KRA PIN to HR. Ask them to confirm your NSSF number. Check your first payslip to see that deductions appear. After two months, check your NSSF statement to confirm the money was actually remitted.
