## SERVICE 7: DRIVING LICENCE (Smart DL)

### What is it
A driving licence is an official document issued by the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) authorizing you to operate a motor vehicle on Kenyan roads. The current format is the Smart Driving Licence (Smart DL) — a credit‑card‑sized card with an embedded chip containing your biometric data and driving history. Driving without a valid licence is a criminal offence.

### Quick Facts
- **Application portal**: serviceportal.ntsa.go.ke or via ecitizen.go.ke
- **Required**: National ID, KRA PIN, passport‑size photo, and completion of training at an NTSA‑registered driving school
- **PDL fee**: KES 600–750 (valid 6 months)
- **Driving test fee**: KES 2,500 (covers theory and practical; retake: KES 2,500 per attempt)
- **Smart DL card fee**: KES 3,050 (3‑year licence)
- **Renewal fees**: 1‑year – KES 650; 3‑year – KES 1,450
- **Replacement fee**: KES 3,050 (lost/stolen/damaged)
- **Processing time**: officially 24–48 hours after biometrics; in practice 3–4 weeks or longer during backlogs
- **Licence status SMS**: send your ID number to **22846**

## PART A: LICENCE CATEGORIES

- Category A – motorcycles
- Category AM – mopeds
- Category B1 – automatic light vehicles
- Category B2 – manual light vehicles
- Category B3 – professional light vehicles
- Category C – heavy commercial vehicles
- Category D1/D2 – public service vehicles (PSV)
- Category E – heavy equipment
- Category F – vehicles for persons with disability
- Category G – industrial, construction, and agricultural machinery

Most Kenyans apply for **Category B2** (manual light vehicles) first. To upgrade to a higher category, you must hold the lower category first and meet experience requirements. For full category specifications, contact NTSA directly or consult the NTSA Service Guide 2025.

## PART B: THE FULL PROCESS – FIRST TIME

Getting a driving licence for the first time involves five stages. Do not skip any.

### Stage 1 – Enrol in a registered driving school
You cannot apply for a licence without completing training at an NTSA‑approved school. Only use NTSA‑registered schools. Ask the school for their NTSA code and verify it at ntsa.go.ke before paying any fees.

**Driving school fees** vary widely: **KES 15,000–25,000** in Nairobi, cheaper in smaller towns, premium schools charge more. This fee is NOT paid to NTSA – it goes directly to the school. Do not pay any school that cannot show you their NTSA registration.

### Stage 2 – Apply for Provisional Driving Licence (PDL)
The PDL allows you to practise driving legally while training.

**How to apply:**
1. Go to https://serviceportal.ntsa.go.ke or access via ecitizen.go.ke
2. Register an account if you don't have one
3. Select **Apply for Provisional Driving Licence**
4. Upload required documents: National ID, passport‑size photo, KRA PIN
5. Pay the fee via M‑Pesa or card (KES 600–750 – confirm exact fee at time of application)
6. Visit the designated NTSA office for biometric capture if required
7. Download and print your PDL

**PDL validity:** 6 months. It can be renewed if it expires before you sit your test.

**IMPORTANT:** Confirm your PDL is actually issued in the TIMS system and linked to your National ID before booking your test. Some agents and driving schools have been known to issue fake or unlinked PDLs. If your PDL is not in TIMS, you cannot sit the test.

### Stage 3 – Book and sit the driving test
The test has two parts – both must be passed.

**Theory test (Model Town Board):** an oral examination using a scale model of road layouts. You demonstrate knowledge of road rules, signs, and right of way. Pass mark: **80%** (16 out of 20 questions).

**Practical test:** a road test assessing vehicle control, observation, manoeuvring, hill starts, parking, and road discipline.

**Common reasons people fail:** not checking mirrors and blind spots, poor clutch control on hill starts, wrong priority and lane discipline on the Model Town Board, poor observation at junctions.

**Test fee:** KES 2,500 (covers both theory and practical in one booking). If you fail and retake: KES 2,500 per attempt.

**How to book:**
1. Log into eCitizen or NTSA TIMS portal
2. Select **Driving Test Booking**
3. Choose a test centre and preferred date
4. Pay the fee via M‑Pesa or bank
5. Download and print the test booking slip

**What to bring on test day:** original National ID, test booking slip, Provisional Driving Licence, and any other documents specified at booking.

**Reality on test slots:** Nairobi Embakasi test centre slots can take weeks to months to secure. Book as early as possible after getting your PDL. Arrive early on test day – queues are long, shade is limited, and facilities are basic at some centres.

**Bribe culture at test centres:** some examiners have been reported to solicit money to pass candidates or secure earlier dates. NTSA conducts periodic crackdowns but enforcement is uneven. Do not pay. If approached, report to NTSA at **0709 932 000** or info@ntsa.go.ke.

### Stage 4 – Interim Driving Licence
Issued immediately after passing both theory and practical tests. Valid for **3 months** or until you collect your Smart DL – whichever comes first.

Download from your TIMS account. Do not go to a separate office. This is a **legally valid document** while you wait for the physical Smart DL card. However – some traffic officers on the road do not accept it and use the confusion to solicit bribes, especially targeting matatu and boda boda operators. Always carry your printed interim licence and stand your ground – it is legally valid.

### Stage 5 – Apply for Smart DL card
1. Log into the NTSA TIMS portal
2. Select **Apply for Smart Driving Licence**
3. Upload: test pass certificate, ID, photo
4. Pay **KES 3,050**
5. Book a biometric capture appointment at an NTSA centre or equipped Huduma Centre
6. Attend biometric appointment: fingerprint capture (all fingers), digital photograph, signature capture, data verified against National ID
7. Receive SMS when the card is ready
8. Collect at the **same centre** where you did biometrics

**Reality on biometric appointments:** Not all Huduma Centres offer Smart DL biometric capture. As of early 2026, only about **15 centres** are equipped. Some only handle collection, not capture. Call or check ntsa.go.ke before travelling. Nairobi CBD and GPO centres fill up fast. Try less busy centres, or check early morning Tuesday to Thursday – avoid Fridays and month‑end.

**Reality on processing time:** Official claim: 24 to 48 hours after biometric capture. Reality: most people wait **3 to 4 weeks**. During backlogs, it stretches much longer. In early 2026, NTSA faced major backlog criticism and the new Director General pledged to clear it. Always check status online before travelling to collect – the card may not be physically there yet even if status says "ready."

## PART C: TOTAL COST – FIRST TIME

**Government fees only:**
- PDL: KES 600–750
- Driving test: KES 2,500
- Smart DL card: KES 3,050
- Total government fees: approximately **KES 6,300**

**Additional real costs:**
- Driving school fees: KES 15,000–25,000+
- Cyber café help for TIMS applications: KES 100–300 per service
- Passport photos: KES 150–300
- Transport to centres (multiple trips): varies
- Test retakes if you fail: KES 2,500 each

**Total realistic first‑time cost:** KES 20,000–35,000+ including driving school.

Any person or broker charging you thousands to "guarantee a pass" or "fast‑track your Smart DL" is likely involved in corruption. Avoid them – you risk losing money, getting a fraudulent licence, and facing criminal charges.

## PART D: RENEWAL

### Who needs to renew
Anyone whose Smart DL has expired or is about to expire. Driving with an expired licence is a criminal offence – fine up to **KES 10,000** or imprisonment. Renew before your expiry date.

### How to renew online
1. Log into the NTSA eCitizen portal
2. Navigate to NTSA Services → **Driving Licence Renewal**
3. Enter: National ID number, driving licence number, phone, and email
4. Select renewal duration and pay:
   - **1‑year renewal: KES 650**
   - **3‑year renewal: KES 1,450**
5. Download and print the renewal slip as temporary proof
6. Collect the new card when notified via SMS

### Payment issues during renewal
This is a known and widespread problem. In early 2026, a major eCitizen outage left thousands unable to pay for renewals. M‑Pesa transactions stayed "pending" for hours or days. NTSA blamed eCitizen; eCitizen blamed M‑Pesa synchronisation delays. Neither took clear responsibility.

**If payment is stuck on "pending":** do not pay again immediately – double payment is hard to reverse. Wait at least 1 to 2 hours and refresh. Log out and log back in. Monitor your M‑Pesa messages. If stuck beyond several hours during a known outage, wait for system restoration before trying again. Contact eCitizen support – not NTSA, as they disown the payment layer.

If system failure causes your licence to expire while you were trying to renew: some NTSA offices issue informal letters confirming renewal is in progress. This is not a formal national policy, but it can help if you are stopped. Keep all M‑Pesa confirmation screenshots as evidence of your attempt.

## PART E: REPLACEMENT – LOST, STOLEN, OR DAMAGED

### Documents needed
- **Police abstract** (for lost or stolen – get this first at the nearest police station)
- Copy of National ID
- Passport‑size photographs
- Payment receipt

Note: an affidavit is not always required. It is mainly requested when details are inconsistent or there is a dispute. A police abstract plus ID is usually enough for a straightforward replacement.

### How to apply
1. Log into NTSA TIMS or eCitizen
2. Select **Duplicate Driving Licence** or **Replacement** service
3. Complete the application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Pay **KES 3,050**
6. Attend biometric verification if required
7. Collect the replacement card when notified

## PART F: UPGRADING YOUR LICENCE CLASS

To add a new vehicle category to your licence:
1. Apply for **Driving Class Endorsement** through the NTSA TIMS portal
2. You must hold the lower class first and meet experience requirements
3. You may need additional training at an approved driving school
4. Pass the practical test for the new class
5. Pay the endorsement fee (contact NTSA for the current amount – not publicly listed)

## PART G: FOREIGN LICENCE CONVERSION

Holders of valid foreign licences from countries with recognised licensing standards may convert without full retesting.

### Documents needed
- Valid foreign driving licence (original and copy)
- Original passport and copies
- Work permit and dependant pass (if applicable)
- Two passport‑size photographs
- Letter of authentication from the embassy or issuing authority
- Valid Alien Card (original and copy)

### How to apply
1. Log into NTSA TIMS via eCitizen
2. Select **Foreign Driving Licence Conversion**
3. Upload all required documents
4. Pay the conversion fee (approximately **KES 1,050**)
5. Wait for NTSA processing (officially 14 to 21 working days)
6. Collect your Kenyan Smart DL upon approval

**Reality for foreign licence holders:** some applicants are told to retake the full driving test despite holding long‑standing foreign licences. This happens when NTSA questions authenticity or category equivalence. Requirements for which countries qualify for full conversion versus retesting are not clearly published – contact NTSA directly before starting.

**Tourists:** can drive with a valid foreign licence for up to **90 days** without needing an International Driving Permit.

## PART H: CHECKING YOUR LICENCE STATUS

- **Method 1 – SMS:** send your ID number to **22846**. Check before travelling to collect.
- **Method 2 – TIMS portal:** log into eCitizen or NTSA TIMS, check under My Applications or dashboard.
- **Method 3 – NTSA call centre:** call **0709 932 000**, Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm.
- **Method 4 – Physical visit:** visit any NTSA office or equipped Huduma Centre with your original ID.

Always confirm online before going to collect – the card may not be physically ready even if status says "ready."

## PART I: DEMERIT POINTS SYSTEM

All drivers start with **20 points**. Points are deducted for traffic violations. Serious offences: 4 to 6 points per offence. Reaching zero points triggers licence suspension or mandatory retraining. Points reset after a violation‑free period.

The second‑generation Smart DL (rolling out 2025 to 2026) will fully integrate demerit tracking into the card and backend system. **Reality:** full demerit enforcement is not yet consistently applied nationwide, even though the technical structure exists.

## PART J: COMMON QUESTIONS

### Swahili
Q: Sijawahi pata leseni. Nianze wapi?
A: Anza kwa kujiandikisha katika shule ya udereva iliyoidhinishwa na NTSA. Uliza namba ya NTSA ya shule na uithibitishe kwenye ntsa.go.ke kabla ya kulipa chochote. Baada ya mafunzo, omba PDL kwenye TIMS, kisha panga mtihani, kisha Smart DL.

Q: PDL ni nini na kwa nini ninahitaji?
A: PDL ni Leseni ya Udereva ya Muda inayokuruhusu kufanya mazoezi ya kuendesha gari kisheria wakati wa mafunzo. Bila PDL, huwezi kukaa mtihani wa NTSA. Ada ni KES 600 hadi 750. Inatumika miezi 6.

Q: Mtihani wa udereva unagharimu ngapi?
A: KES 2,500 kwa nadharia na vitendo pamoja. Ukishindwa na ukarudia, utalipia KES 2,500 tena kwa kila jaribio.

Q: Ninashindwa kupata nafasi ya mtihani Embakasi. Nifanye nini?
A: Vituo vya Nairobi vinajaa haraka sana. Angalia vituo vingine kama Huduma Centres zilizo nje ya CBD. Angalia TIMS mapema asubuhi Jumanne hadi Alhamisi – nafasi mpya zinaweza kufunguka wakati huo.

Q: Smart DL inagharimu ngapi?
A: KES 3,050 kwa leseni ya miaka 3. Hii ni ada ya serikali tu – haijumuishi ada ya shule ya udereva.

Q: Baada ya biometrics, nangoja muda gani?
A: Rasmi: masaa 24 hadi 48. Ukweli: wiki 3 hadi 4 mara nyingi. Wakati wa msongamano inaweza kuchukua zaidi ya hapo. Angalia hali kwenye TIMS au tuma SMS kwenda 22846 kabla ya kwenda kukusanya.

Q: Leseni yangu ya muda (interim) inakubaliwa barabarani?
A: Ndiyo kisheria – ni hati halisi unaposubiri Smart DL. Hata hivyo, baadhi ya askari hawaikubali na hutumia hali hii kudai rushwa, hasa kwa madereva wa matatu na bodaboda. Beba kopi yako iliyochapishwa na simama imara – ni halali chini ya sheria.

Q: Malipo yangu yanaonyesha "pending" na hayaendi mbele. Nifanye nini?
A: Usilipie tena mara moja. Subiri saa 1 hadi 2, kisha refresh na ingia tena. Hifadhi picha ya uthibitisho wa M‑Pesa. Ikiwa mtandao una tatizo linalojulikana, subiri mfumo kurudi kabla ya kujaribu tena. NTSA haitashughulikia tatizo la malipo – wasiliana na msaada wa eCitizen badala yake.

Q: Leseni yangu imepotea. Nifanye nini?
A: Nenda polisi kwanza upate police abstract. Kisha ingia TIMS, chagua Duplicate Driving Licence, pakia nyaraka, lipa KES 3,050, na ufanye biometrics tena.

Q: Ninarenew leseni yangu. Ada ni ngapi?
A: Mwaka 1: KES 650. Miaka 3: KES 1,450. Fanya mtandaoni kupitia eCitizen au TIMS.

### English
Q: I've never had a driving licence. Where do I start?
A: First, enrol in an NTSA‑registered driving school. Ask for the school's NTSA code and verify it at ntsa.go.ke before paying. After training, apply for your PDL via TIMS, then book your test, then apply for the Smart DL.

Q: What is a PDL and why do I need it?
A: The Provisional Driving Licence lets you practise driving legally while training. You cannot sit the NTSA test without it. It costs KES 600–750 and is valid for 6 months.

Q: How much does the driving test cost?
A: KES 2,500 for both theory and practical in one booking. If you fail and retake, it's KES 2,500 per attempt.

Q: I can't get a test slot at Embakasi. What can I do?
A: Nairobi slots fill up very fast. Check other centres, including Huduma Centres outside the CBD. Check TIMS early morning Tuesday to Thursday – new slots may appear then.

Q: How much does the Smart DL cost?
A: KES 3,050 for a 3‑year licence. This is the government fee only – it does not include driving school fees.

Q: After biometrics, how long do I wait for the card?
A: Officially: 24–48 hours. In reality: 3–4 weeks most of the time. During backlogs, it can take much longer. Check your status on TIMS or SMS your ID to 22846 before going to collect.

Q: Is my interim driving licence valid on the road?
A: Yes, legally – it is a valid document while you wait for the Smart DL card. However, some traffic officers do not accept it and use the confusion to solicit bribes. Carry your printed copy and stand your ground – it is legally valid.

Q: My payment is stuck on "pending". What should I do?
A: Do not pay again immediately. Wait 1–2 hours, refresh, and log back in. Save your M‑Pesa confirmation screenshot. If the system is down, wait for restoration before retrying. Contact eCitizen support – not NTSA – for payment issues.

Q: My licence was lost. What do I do?
A: Get a police abstract first. Then log into TIMS, select Duplicate Driving Licence, upload documents, pay KES 3,050, and attend biometrics again.

Q: How much does renewal cost?
A: 1‑year: KES 650. 3‑year: KES 1,450. Renew online via eCitizen or TIMS before your licence expires.

## PART K: OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

- **Driving without a valid licence:** fine up to **KES 10,000** or imprisonment.
- **Driving under influence (DUI):** fine up to **KES 500,000** and/or imprisonment up to **10 years** under Traffic Act Section 44.
- **Reckless driving:** fines from **KES 20,000 to KES 100,000**.
- **Speeding:** fines from **KES 10,000 to KES 100,000** depending on severity.
- **Using a fake licence or fraudulent application:** criminal prosecution under the NTSA Act and Traffic Act, potential imprisonment and fines, permanent disqualification from holding a licence.
- **Demerit points reaching zero:** licence suspension or mandatory retraining.

## PART L: RECENT CHANGES 2025–2026

- **Second‑generation Smart DL rollout:** NTSA is partnering with KCB Bank and Pesa Print Limited under a 21‑year PPP. New cards are secure five‑layer polycarbonate with an embedded chip. Production capacity is approximately 5 million cards every three years.
- **Mobile Driving Licence (MDL) wallet:** drivers will be able to access licence details on mobile phones, and pay licence fees and traffic fines via mobile money, USSD, and banking.
- **Expanded centres:** over 102 registration centres planned nationwide with 390+ enrolment kits. As of early 2026, only about 15 centres are fully operational.
- **Demerit system integration:** full integration with the second‑generation Smart DL is coming, automating tracking of all traffic violations.
- **Early 2026 backlog crisis:** NTSA faced widespread criticism over Smart DL and number plate processing delays. The new Director General, Nashon Kundiwa, pledged to clear the backlog. NTSA sent bulk SMS notifications between February 25 and March 2, 2026, to drivers whose cards were ready. The situation remains uneven.
